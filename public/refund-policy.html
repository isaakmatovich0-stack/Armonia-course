Refund & Returns Policy

Last updated: November 6, 2026

Armonía Connect is a digital product. The moment your payment is confirmed, you receive an access code that immediately unlocks the full course — every lesson, recording, and resource included with your purchase.

What Your Purchase Covers

Your $100 payment covers full access to this specific course, built around the current season's All-State Audition Etudes. It does not include future seasons' courses, which are released as separate purchases when new etudes are announced.

Course Purchases: All Sales Are Final

Because course materials are digital and accessible instantly upon purchase, all sales are final. We do not offer refunds once an access code has been issued and used to log in.

Before You Buy

If you have any questions about what's included, which instruments are covered, or whether this course is right for you, reach out before purchasing — we're happy to help you decide. Use the contact form on the site or email us directly.

Store Orders: 30-Day Returns

This is different from the course policy above. Merchandise bought from the shop is made and shipped by our fulfilment partner, and has a 30-day return window from delivery.

Damaged, misprinted, or the wrong item: send a photo within 30 days and we will replace it free or refund you — your choice. You do not need to post it back.

Changed your mind, or the size is off: unworn, unused items in their original packaging can be returned within 30 days. Returns go to our fulfilment partner's US facility, never overseas. Return postage is yours to cover, and we refund the item within 5 business days of it arriving.

What we cannot take back: opened sticker sets, caps that have been worn or lost their tags, and pick sets — a $16 pick set costs more to post back across the Pacific than it is worth, so those are covered for defects and errors only.

The shipping & returns page has the full detail, including how to start a return.

Billing Issues

If you were charged in error, charged more than once, or believe there's a genuine billing mistake, contact us right away and we'll work with you to resolve it.
