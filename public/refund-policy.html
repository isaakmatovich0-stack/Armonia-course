Refund Policy

Last updated: August 18, 2026

Armonía Connect is a digital product. The moment your payment is confirmed, you receive an access code that immediately unlocks the full course — every lesson, recording, and resource included with your purchase.

What Your Purchase Covers

Your $100 payment covers full access to this specific course, built around the current season's All-State Audition Etudes. It does not include future seasons' courses, which are released as separate purchases when new etudes are announced.

All Sales Are Final

Because course materials are digital and accessible instantly upon purchase, all sales are final. We do not offer refunds once an access code has been issued and used to log in.

Before You Buy

If you have any questions about what's included, which instruments are covered, or whether this course is right for you, reach out before purchasing — we're happy to help you decide. Use the contact form on the site or email us directly.

Billing Issues

If you were charged in error, charged more than once, or believe there's a genuine billing mistake, contact us right away and we'll work with you to resolve it.
