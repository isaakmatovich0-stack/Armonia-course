Children's Privacy

Armonía Connect is built for students who may be under 18. We rely on a parent or guardian to complete the purchase and be aware of the account. If you believe a child under 13 has given us information without a guardian's knowledge, contact us and we'll delete it.

Your Rights

You can request a copy of your data, ask us to correct it, or request deletion of your account at any time.
