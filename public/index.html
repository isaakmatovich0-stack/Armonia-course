Frequently Asked Questions

What exactly do I get for $100? +

Full access to this year's course: every etude breakdown and lesson, MIDI and live play-along tracks, chord books, mock audition simulations, and access to the Armonía Connect Studio to message me directly and connect with other students. It's a one-time payment — no subscription.

What instrument(s) does this course cover? +

Vihuela, Guitarra, Guitarra de Golpe, and Guitarrón — including a professionally recorded performance of each etude and lessons for each instrument. Play-along tracks and MIDI files are included for every instrument as well.

When do I get access after purchasing? +

Instantly. As soon as your payment is confirmed, you'll receive an access code by email that logs you into the Armonía Connect Studio, where all course materials are waiting.

Does this course cover future etude seasons too? +

No — this course is built specifically around the 2026–27 All-State Audition Etudes. When a new season's etudes are released, a new course covering that repertoire will be created as a separate purchase.

Can I ask questions after I enroll? +

Yes. Once you're enrolled, you can message me directly through the Armonía Connect Studio any time you have a question about a lesson or your progress.

How is this different from private lessons? +

Private lessons are great, but they're expensive and hard to schedule around a full course load. This gives you the same measure-by-measure attention to detail — recorded, structured, and available whenever you have time to practice — at a fraction of the cost.

What device do I need to use this course? +

Any of them. The course works on desktop, phone, tablet, or TV — log in from wherever you're practicing.

What's your refund policy? +

See our full Refund Policy for details. If you have any questions before purchasing, reach out via the contact form and we're happy to help you decide.

Who Am I?

Isaak
Matovich

Professional Vihuelista, multi-instrumentalist, and the 2025–2026 TMEA All-State vihuela player.

Having the chance to represent all of Texas as the #1 vihuela player showed me exactly what it takes to reach that level, and I created Armonía Connect to share the specific preparatory steps that got me there. Alongside mariachi, I've played classical violin for seven and a half years, serving as concertmaster of my high school orchestras, and that discipline shapes how I teach — blending authentic mariachi style with real technical foundation.
