## 1. What This Policy Covers

This Privacy Policy explains what information Armonía Connect ("we," "us," "our") collects when you purchase or use our course website, Armonía Connect Portal, and related services (the "Service"), how we use it, and who we share it with.

## 2. Information We Collect

**2.1 Information you provide directly:**
- Name and email address (collected at checkout via Stripe, and again if you complete your student profile)
- Billing address (collected by Stripe during payment)
- Profile information you choose to add: instrument, experience level, years playing, a short bio, and a profile photo
- Messages you send to the instructor or, if enabled, to the student community
- Any information you include in a contact form submission

**2.2 Information collected automatically:**
- Login activity tied to your access code (timestamps, and a hashed — not raw — version of your IP address, used only to detect access-code sharing)
- Your browser's local storage, which holds your session token so you stay logged in between visits

**2.3 Payment information.** We do not collect or store your full credit card number. Payment is processed entirely by **Stripe**, and is subject to [Stripe's Privacy Policy](https://stripe.com/privacy). We receive only your name, email, billing address, and confirmation that payment succeeded.

## 3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:
- Create and manage your access code and course access
- Send your access code and order confirmation by email
- Personalize your experience (e.g., displaying your profile, tailoring guidance based on your instrument and level)
- Respond to messages you send us
- Detect and prevent access-code sharing or other violations of our Terms and Conditions
- Improve the course and communicate updates, such as new lessons or announcements
- Comply with legal obligations

We do not sell your personal information to third parties, and we do not use your data for third-party advertising.

## 4. Who We Share Information With

We share limited information with the third-party services that power Armonía Connect, each acting as a data processor on our behalf:

| Service | What it's used for | What's shared |
|---|---|---|
| **Stripe** | Payment processing | Name, email, billing address, payment details |
| **Supabase** | Database (stores your access code, profile, and messages) | Name, email, profile info, messages, login activity |
| **Resend** | Sending confirmation and notification emails | Name, email |
| **Vercel** | Hosting the website and backend | Standard web request data (e.g., IP address, in server logs) |
| **Soundslice** *(if/when integrated)* | Displaying interactive sheet music and performance tracks | No personal account data is shared with Soundslice; it is embedded content only |

Each of these providers has its own privacy policy governing how they handle data on our behalf. We choose providers that offer strong security practices, but we cannot control their systems directly.

We may also disclose information if required by law, to protect our legal rights (including enforcing our Terms and Conditions against access-code sharing or copyright infringement), or in connection with a business transfer (such as a sale of Armonía Connect).

## 5. Children's Privacy

Armonía Connect is designed for students preparing for programs like TMEA All-State, many of whom are under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children under 13 without a parent or guardian completing the purchase and being aware of the account. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child under 13 has provided us with personal information without your consent, contact us at the email below and we will delete it.

## 6. Data Security

We take reasonable technical measures to protect your information, including using reputable, security-conscious third-party providers (Stripe, Supabase) and restricting database access to server-side systems only. However, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 7. Data Retention

We retain your account and profile information for as long as your access code remains active, and for a reasonable period afterward for record-keeping, dispute resolution, and legal compliance. You may request deletion of your account data at any time (see Section 8).

## 8. Your Rights

You may:
- Request a copy of the personal information we hold about you
- Request correction of inaccurate information (e.g., by editing your profile directly)
- Request deletion of your account and associated data, subject to our need to retain certain records for legal or accounting purposes
- Ask questions about how your data is used

To exercise any of these rights, email **maestro.armoniaconnect@gmail.com**.

## 9. Cookies and Local Storage

We use your browser's local storage (not third-party advertising cookies) to keep you signed in between visits by storing your session token. Clearing your browser's local storage will sign you out.
